# Arbitrary-length generalized theta pairs

`ArbitraryLengthTheta.lean` extends the public length-two theta and facial-subdivision development to arbitrary finite branch-length profiles. All constructions and proofs are finite combinatorics over the existing `GraphicDualCertificate` interface.

## Finite subdivision chains

`Chain P Q` records an actual finite sequence of `LabelledDualPair.subdivPair` operations, with final pair `Q`.

* `Chain.origin c` maps every current label to the original label whose series contains it; `Chain.embed c` is a right inverse on original labels.
* `Chain.segmentCount c e` counts the segments generated by `e`. `Chain.card_origin_fibre` proves that this is literally `Fintype.card {f : Q.Edge // origin c f = e}`.
* `Chain.faceEquiv` identifies final and original faces. `dualSource_faceEquiv_origin` and `dualTarget_faceEquiv_origin` show that each current facial edge has exactly the facial endpoints of its origin.
* `Chain.subdivOld`, `Chain.ofList`, and `Chain.ofCounts` execute requested finite subdivision counts. `segmentCount_ofCounts` gives the exact formula `1 + n e`.
* `Chain.graphicDualCertificate` transports matroid duality, nonempty faces, and all-open facial connectivity along any chain.

## Generalized theta families

For `G.IsLabelledCycle`, branch labels are `G.Edge` and `L : G.Edge → ℕ`.

* `positiveThetaPair G L` is the definitive arbitrary-positive-length family. It starts at `parallelPair G`, whose primal has one direct edge in each branch and whose facial dual is `G`, and subdivides branch `i` exactly `L i - 1` times.
* `positiveTheta_card_origin_fibre` proves, under `1 ≤ L i`, that the actual origin-fibre of branch `i` has cardinality `L i`. Length-one branches are included.
* `positiveTheta_dualSource_origin` and `positiveTheta_dualTarget_origin` identify every facial edge with the parallel copy of its original cycle edge under `positiveThetaFaceEquiv`.
* `positiveThetaPair_graphicDualCertificate` gives exact cycle-matroid duality, nonempty faces, and all-open facial preconnectedness. `positiveThetaPair_dual_cycleMatroid` exposes the matroid equality separately.
* `thetaPairOfLengths G L` is the alternative construction starting from the public `lengthTwoThetaPair G`. It subdivides only `(i,false)` by `L i - 2`; `thetaChain_branchLength` proves the two origin-fibres have total count `L i` for `2 ≤ L i`. This compatibility family is retained because it directly consumes the existing length-two theorem.

The only graph premise for either certificate is the existing connected labelled-cycle hypothesis `G.IsLabelledCycle`; no arbitrary-planarity or matroid-duality premise is introduced. Positivity is needed for the numerical branch-length statements, not for preservation of the combinatorial certificate itself.

## Scope

This is an exact labelled-dual-pair construction with its combinatorial graphic-duality certificate. It does not assert a continuous planar drawing, Jordan separation, matroidal independence beyond what the certificate supplies, or any infinite uniqueness result. The open-path/cycle semantics needed for a later Proposition 4.2 application remain to be transported separately; the present result supplies the finite arbitrary-length graphs and their bundled-cycle duals.
